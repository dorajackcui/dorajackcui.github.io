## Japanese names and wordplay

The Japanese names and Bardakoff's explanations were checked against his notes in [Libération's Pokémon feature](https://www.liberation.fr/apps/2016/06/pokemon/) on 2026-09-15. The article attributes the imagined Fushigidane/Fushigisō exchange, Hitokage's shadow association, Zenigame's small size, Kamex's monster-name sound and Kyūkon's fox cry to Bardakoff rather than presenting his readings as statements from the Japanese naming team. The official [Japanese Pokédex](https://zukan.pokemon.co.jp/detail/0038) confirms the spelling キュウコン (the feature uses a half-width character in this name).

The duck-and-leek proverb is explained with [Kanjipedia's dictionary entry](https://www.kanjipedia.jp/kotoba/0000536100): it describes a conveniently profitable situation, not simply the duck putting itself in danger. Both the Japanese wording and the Chinese gloss appear in the article.
